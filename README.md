# 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Data Analyst** passionate about transforming raw data into meaningful business insights.

My professional journey began at **Wipro**, where I worked on a UK Telecom project involving operational reporting, KPI tracking, stakeholder communication, and ServiceNow incident analysis. Working closely with business operations inspired me to move beyond solving issues and start uncovering the patterns behind them.

Today, I'm focused on building practical analytics projects that combine business understanding, SQL, Python, Power BI, and data visualization to support better decision-making.

- 📊 Data Analytics & Business Intelligence
- 📈 KPI Reporting & Dashboard Development
- 📉 Data Visualization & Storytelling
- 🧹 Data Cleaning & Transformation
- 🤖 AI-powered Analytics
- 📚 Continuous Learner
